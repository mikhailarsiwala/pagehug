# Page Keepers

create a website for me based on my product of magnetic bookmarks, give it a catchy name Create a modern, premium e-commerce website for a brand that sells **magnetic bookmarks**.

### Brand Concept

The product is a stylish magnetic bookmark that folds over the page and uses two small magnets to securely hold the bookmark in place without damaging the book. The brand should feel **aesthetic, youthful, premium, and giftable**, appealing especially to students, readers, book lovers, and people who like stationery.

### Overall Design

* Clean, minimalist, premium UI

* Warm cream/off-white background

* Elegant typography mixed with a modern sans-serif

* Subtle animations and smooth scrolling

* High-quality product photography

* Rounded cards and buttons

* Lots of whitespace

* Make the website feel like a real premium D2C brand, not a generic template

* Mobile-first and fully responsive

* Use tasteful micro-interactions when hovering over products and buttons

### Why Magnetic?

Create a visually interesting section explaining the product:

**"Small Bookmark. Big Upgrade."**

Show 3–4 benefits:

1. **Stays in Place** — Strong magnetic closure keeps your page securely marked.

2. **No More Falling Out** — Unlike traditional bookmarks, it won't slip out of your book.

3. **Book-Friendly** — Compact and lightweight, designed to avoid damaging pages.

4. **Looks Good** — Functional stationery that adds personality to your reading experience.
